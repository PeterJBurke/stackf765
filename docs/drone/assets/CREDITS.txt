StackF765 drone / board viewer (docs/drone/) - credits and licences
====================================================================

Flight controller boards
  The three boards shown (lower power board, upper flight-controller board, service board) are the
  GLB models exported by kicad-cli from the KiCad design files in this repository
  (https://github.com/PeterJBurke/stackf765). They are loaded from ../boards/ and are not copied here.
  Hardware licence: CERN-OHL-P v2 (see the project page).

Page design
  Layout, controls, camera presets and the frame context are modelled on Peter Burke's FC viewer at
  https://peterjburke.github.io/dronerenders/fc/ (repository PeterJBurke/dronerenders).

Frame model: assets/frame-mod-l-hd-cargo.js
  MOD-L 4-inch HD Cargo frame (4" regular arms, Cargo top and bottom plates, cross plate, 30 mm standoffs).
  Design: MOD-L by ledroneclub, Thingiverse thing 4295478, https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4295478
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY), as stated with the designer's files.
  The packed mesh file is copied unchanged from dronerenders fc/assets/native-frame.js, where the
  designer's part geometry was assembled and recoloured for that viewer (parts not stretched).
  Change made here: the frame's two middle standoffs (of six; the pair beside the tail end of the
  flight controller) are not drawn. They are skipped when the page loads the meshes
  (OMITTED_FRAME_MESHES in assets/stackf765.js); the mesh file is not edited. The plates keep
  their holes for those standoffs.

Scale coin: assets/penny.js (copied unchanged from dronerenders fc/assets/penny.js)
