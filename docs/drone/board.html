StackF765 boards

This render is generated from the KiCad design files in the stackf765 repository: the boards are the GLB models that kicad-cli exports for the project page, loaded here unmodified.

  • Lower: power board, 31.5 × 48 mm, four 4 mm holes on a 20 × 20 mm pattern for rubber grommets, battery / ESC solder pads at the tail end, 3 × 9 motor header (signal / 5 V / ground per column) at the front.
  • Upper: flight controller (STM32F765), same outline, top surface 9.6 mm above the lower board's (8.0 mm standoffs + 1.6 mm PCB).
  • Service: USB, buzzer and boot button, 18 × 32 mm, on a 100 mm 6-wire cable (cable not drawn on this page).

The frame posts, grommets, washers and screws under the stack are simple illustrative shapes, not part of the KiCad export. Scale: 10 mm grid, a US cent (Ø19.05 mm) and a 70 mm rule.
