StackF765
in a MOD-L 4″ HD Cargo frame

The StackF765 flight controller stack, shown where it mounts in a 4-inch quadcopter frame, with its service board loose on its cable. The three boards in this render are generated from the KiCad design files in the stackf765 repository: they are the same GLB models that kicad-cli exports for the project page, loaded here unmodified.

StackF765 stack
Two 31.5 × 48 mm boards, long axis fore-aft. Lower = power board, upper = flight controller (STM32F765). Upper top surface is 9.6 mm above the lower top surface (8.0 mm standoffs + 1.6 mm PCB). Rev B: the upper 3 × 12 and lower 3 × 9 right-angle pin headers face the nose, their bodies overhanging the front edge; the four battery / ESC solder pads are at the tail end of the lower board.
Rubber grommet mounting
Soft-mounted, Matek style: a silicone grommet is pressed into each of the lower board's four 4 mm holes (20 × 20 mm pattern) and an M2 screw with a washer goes through each grommet into the frame, so the lower board floats on rubber 2.5 mm above the plate. The upper board is not fastened to the frame; it sits on the lower board's three standoffs. View “Mounting” shows the grommets side-on. The orange grommets (generic “M3 to M2” type: 4 mm waist, 6.6 mm flanges, 6.6 mm tall), washers and screws are simple illustrative shapes, not part of the KiCad export; frame-side nuts are not drawn.
Service board
Service board (USB / buzzer / boot) is on a 100 mm cable and can be mounted anywhere on the airframe; shown loose. It is an 18 × 32 mm board on a 6-wire cable from the right-hand edge of the upper board, drawn lying on the ground beside the frame; the cable route is an illustration.
Frame: MOD-L 4″ HD Cargo
4″ regular arms, Cargo top and bottom plates, cross plate, 30 mm standoffs. Frame design: MOD-L by ledroneclub, Thingiverse thing 4295478, licensed Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY). The frame meshes were assembled and recoloured from the designer's files for the dronerenders FC viewer, which this page is modelled on.

Roof hidden to show the flight controller.

Frame and flight controller only: no motors, propellers, ESC, battery or wiring are shown. Fit has not been checked on hardware. Scale: the grid is 10 mm (heavy lines 50 mm) and the coin is a US cent, 19.05 mm across.
