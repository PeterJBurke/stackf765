# Pre-fabrication audit, 2026-11-19

Scope: pin-by-pin datasheet audit of every IC, transistor, diode, crystal and connector on the
upper, lower and service boards; part number vs footprint; ratings; UNVERIFIED items from
`research/*.md` and `docs/verification.md`; analogue calculations; `hwdef.dat` sanity.

Method: datasheets were fetched fresh (TI from ti.com; others as the manufacturer PDF hosted by
LCSC, or from the manufacturer site). Pad-to-net maps were read from the three `.kicad_pcb` files
with `pcbnew`, not from the generator sources. The pin tables in `research/*.md` were not used as
a source. `ngspice` is not installed, so the analogue checks are numerical (Python). Nothing in
the design was modified. Three reviewers worked in parallel (ICs / firmware / calculations;
connectors; discretes and passives); this is the merged report.

Evidence tags: **[MFR]** manufacturer datasheet or drawing actually opened, **[DIST]** distributor
record (LCSC product API), **[PCB]** read from the board file, **[INF]** inferred or calculated.

**Result: no BLOCKER was found.** Every pin map, land pattern and polarity that could be checked
against a manufacturer document is correct. There are 2 MAJOR findings and a set of MINOR ones.

## 3. Calculations

`ngspice` is not installed; these are closed-form results (script kept in the audit scratch area).

### 3.1 TPS62933 5 V regulators (datasheet section 10.2 equations)

- Output: 0.8 V x (1 + 53.2 k / 10 k) = 5.056 V. With 1% resistors 4.97-5.14 V; adding VFB 784-816 mV, 4.87-5.25 V. The AT7456E maximum is 5.25 V and LM66200's is 5.5 V: inside, with no margin at the very top for the OSD.
- Inductor ripple, 4.7 uH, 500 kHz: 0.34 A at 6 V in, 0.86 A at 8.4 V, 1.29 A at 12.6 V (K = 0.43), 1.71 A during a 24.4 V clamp. Peak at 3 A load: 3.64 A at 12.6 V; 3.81 A with inductance 20% low. Minimum high-side limit 4.2 A; inductor Isat 5.0 A typ. Equation 16 with K = 0.4 at 13 V gives 5.2 uH.
- Output capacitor: ripple 15 mV at 22 uF effective. Equation 21 for a 1.5 A step and 250 mV gives 24.6 uF; the fitted 21 uF effective is slightly under, so a 1.5 A step moves the output a little more than 5%.
- Input: 0.19-0.30 Vpp at 5-8 uF effective and 3 A (equation 23); capacitor RMS current up to 1.47 A.
- Soft-start: 33 nF x 0.8 V / 5.5 uA = 4.8 ms (4.1-5.9 ms).
- Dropout at 2S: maximum duty before foldback 93%; needs about 5.4 V in for 5.06 V at 3 A, so regulation holds to an empty 2S pack.
- Loss at 3 A: about 0.8 W per IC and 0.45 W per inductor.

### 3.2 Current sense

- 0.5 mohm x 100 = 50 mV/A; gain error factor 0.9911 with 10 ohm filter resistors; 2.478 V at 50 A.
- Offset +/-60 uV referred to input = +/-0.12 A. Dead zone 0.1 A typ / 1 A max. Linear to 62 A (worst case), clips below 3.3 V so the ADC pin cannot be overdriven.
- Input filter corner 80 kHz; ADC filter 1 k / 100 nF = 1.6 kHz. Shunt 1.25 W at 50 A (rated 6 W).

### 3.3 Battery divider

- 11:1 gives 0.764 V at 8.4 V and 1.145 V at 12.6 V; 2.22 V during a 24.4 V clamp. 8.9 mV of battery per ADC count.
- Source resistance 909 ohm plus 1 k, with 100 nF at the pin: the ADC sample capacitor charges from the 100 nF, so sampling time is not a constraint. 1 uA of pin leakage is 21 mV of battery.

### 3.4 LM66200 OR-ing

- Both inputs within 1.6-5.5 V. The higher input wins; reverse-current blocking trips at 42 mV typ (70 mV max) of VOUT above VIN, response 2 us; switchover about 8 us at 5 V. On-resistance 37 mohm typ: 15 mV at 0.4 A.
- If the USB source is above the buck output (5.06 V), the board runs from USB even with a battery fitted.

### 3.6 Buzzer driver

- On-current 5 V / (10 + 16 ohm) = 192 mA; 3.07 V across the coil (operating range 2.5-4.5 V). The datasheet's 90 mA maximum is quoted at the rated 3.6 Vp-p square wave, so the peak here is above that figure while the average at 50% duty is 96 mA.

### 3.7 Others
